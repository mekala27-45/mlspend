# Explain A Bill

Read AGENTS.md and results/expanded/manifest.json. Resolve the requested team, month, book and method; do not mix direct forecast spend with fully loaded statements. Read the matching allocation, units, idle and incident rows. Reconcile the team statement to its direct and shared parts and the full month to the source total. Compare policies using the same cost population. Link the generated statement and source digest. State simulated versus measured usage and shadow versus paid cost. Report unavailable inputs explicitly. Do not fetch fresh prices into a locked historical calculation or invent a cash-saving claim. Verify every quoted number against the manifest before responding.
