# Cost ownership begins with the allocation rule

mlspend is a portfolio demonstration. Its platform costs are simulated with known truth. Its portfolio costs are shadow estimates of public project usage at published list prices; nobody paid these amounts. No figure is a quote, an invoice or financial advice.

The latest simulated period is 2025-12. The source dataset contains 140160 charge rows. Every source amount is reconciled against the warehouse at team, model, month and day grains with no tolerance in the integer money units.

The largest change across allocation methods is $471.88. An allocation policy changes who pays without changing the total. Idle capacity is therefore reported separately before overhead is distributed.

The FinOps objection is valid: list prices do not equal a negotiated invoice. This report uses recorded price excerpts and labels the scenario. Portfolio usage remains not measured until the authenticated fetch has run.

The platform engineering objection is also valid: releasing a reservation can move cost into idle without reducing the cloud bill. Recommendations report capacity value and leave cash savings unmeasured unless node release is established.
